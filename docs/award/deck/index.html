SVC — Security Video Conference

A fully self-hosted, encrypted video-conferencing and secure document-exchange platform for government bodies and privacy-critical organizations — ending dependence on foreign cloud services like Zoom and Google Meet.

🏛️ Self-hosted ⚡ Elixir / Phoenix 🎥 LiveKit SFU 📱 Android · 🖥 Windows · 🍏 macOS — live
PROBLEM

Why SVC is needed

☁️
Foreign services are unsafeZoom / Meet store data on overseas servers — unsuitable for state secrets and confidential information.
📑
Document leakageFiles sent over unprotected channels fall out of the organization's control.
🕵️
No accountabilityIf no one records who viewed what, there is no responsibility trail.
⚖️
Legal non-complianceConflicts with data-localization requirements for personal and state data.
SOLUTION

One platform — full control

SVC keeps all data and infrastructure inside the organization's own perimeter, with no dependency on any foreign cloud.

🔒
Encrypted video & audioMedia encrypted in transit (WebRTC / DTLS-SRTP); end-to-end (SFrame) on the roadmap.
📦
Secure document exchangeEncrypted storage, versioning, expiry and revocation.
📝
Immutable auditEvery sensitive action recorded in an append-only log.
👥
Role-based access & privacyStrict role hierarchy; meetings visible only to their organizer and invited members.

AN HONEST POSITION

External recording: a realistic answer

⚠️ A physical limit

No software or AI can fully block someone recording the screen with a second phone or microphone. So we rely not on prevention alone, but on detection and traceability.

1
Dynamic watermark — identifies the leaker through a forensic trace
2
On-device capture blocking where the OS allows it (Android / Windows)
3
Organizational control — supervised rooms, device policy
